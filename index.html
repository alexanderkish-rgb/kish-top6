The countdown

Six albums, worst to best

These albums stood out for their creativity, production, songwriting, and achievements throughout 2026.

  1. 06

    Number six: The Romantic

    Bruno Mars

    Released in February 2026, The Romantic is Bruno Mars' fourth solo studio album. It debuted at number one on the Billboard 200, earning 186,000 equivalent album units in its first week. The album also topped Billboard's Top Streaming Albums chart, while its hit single "I Just Might" reached number one on the Hot 100. The project brings back Bruno's signature mix of soul, romance, and retro pop.

  2. 05

    Number five: Visitor

    Sienna Spiro

    Visitor is Sienna Spiro's debut studio album, released in July 2026. The ten-track project explores heartbreak, temporary relationships, and the fear of losing people. Before the album's release, three of her singles, "Die on This Hill," "The Visitor," and "You Stole the Show," had all appeared on the Billboard Hot 100. Her powerful vocals and emotional songwriting helped establish her as one of pop's rising stars.

  3. 04

    Number four: HADES

    Melanie Martinez

    Released in March 2026, HADES is Melanie Martinez's fourth studio album. The 18-track project explores control, exploitation, and the darker sides of society through alternative pop and experimental production. The album reportedly earned around 11 million Spotify streams on its opening day, making it one of her strongest streaming debuts. Songs like "POSSESSION" and "DISNEY PRINCESS" helped introduce its dystopian concept.

  4. 03

    Number three: THIS MUSIC MAY CONTAIN HOPE.

    RAYE

    Released in March 2026, this is RAYE's second studio album. The 17-track project blends jazz, soul, R&B, and pop while exploring heartbreak, healing, and personal growth. It became RAYE's first number-one album on the UK Official Albums Chart and also topped the UK's Albums Streaming Chart. Featuring "WHERE IS MY HUSBAND!" and a collaboration with Hans Zimmer, the album shows her versatility as a singer and songwriter.

  5. 02

    Number two: you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love

    Olivia Rodrigo

    Released in June 2026, this is Olivia Rodrigo's third studio album, following SOUR and GUTS. The 14-track project explores love, uncertainty, and the complicated emotions that come with growing up. Reuniting with producer Dan Nigro, Olivia continues developing her emotional songwriting and pop-rock sound. The album marks another major chapter in her career, showing a more mature perspective on relationships.

  6. 01

    Album of the Year

    Number one: PRIMA

    ADÉLA

    PRIMA is ADÉLA's debut studio album, released in September 2026, and it quickly became her biggest career milestone. The album reached number one in the Netherlands and Belgium, number two in the UK, and the top ten in ten countries. It also topped Spotify's Global and US Top Albums Debut charts. By October 2, the album had surpassed 400 million Spotify streams. Its breakout single "Ain't In LA" reached the Billboard Hot 100 and climbed to number 57, helping introduce ADÉLA to a much larger international audience. With bold production, confident lyrics, and an unforgettable sound, PRIMA earns my number one spot.

Method

How I ranked these albums

What I judged on

I ranked these albums based on their production, songwriting, creativity, replay value, and overall impact.

What I ignored

Streaming numbers and chart positions were not the only factors. I focused on which albums I personally enjoyed listening to the most.

Where I could be wrong

Music is subjective, and everyone has different favorites. Even though some albums had bigger commercial success, my ranking reflects my personal taste.
