The countdown

Six albums, worst to best

These albums stood out for their creativity, production, songwriting, and achievements throughout 2026.

  1. 06

    Number six: The Romantic

    Bruno Mars

    The Romantic is Bruno Mars' fourth solo studio album, bringing back his signature mix of soul, romance, and retro pop. The album features smooth vocals, catchy melodies, and a nostalgic sound that highlights his musical style.

  2. 05

    Number five: Visitor

    Sienna Spiro

    Visitor is Sienna Spiro's debut studio album, exploring heartbreak, relationships, and the fear of losing people. Her powerful vocals and emotional songwriting make the album feel personal and memorable.

  3. 04

    Number four: HADES

    Melanie Martinez

    HADES is Melanie Martinez's fourth studio album, exploring themes of control, exploitation, and the darker sides of society. The album combines alternative pop with experimental production to create a dark and unique sound.

  4. 03

    Number three: THIS MUSIC MAY CONTAIN HOPE.

    RAYE

    THIS MUSIC MAY CONTAIN HOPE. is RAYE's second studio album, blending jazz, soul, R&B, and pop. The album explores heartbreak, healing, and personal growth while highlighting her powerful vocals and versatility.

  5. 02

    Number two: you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love

    Olivia Rodrigo

    you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love is Olivia Rodrigo's third studio album, following SOUR and GUTS. The album explores love, uncertainty, and growing up through emotional lyrics and her signature pop-rock sound.

  6. 01

    Album of the Year

    Number one: PRIMA

    ADÉLA

    PRIMA is ADÉLA's debut studio album, featuring bold production, confident lyrics, and an energetic pop sound. The album highlights her unique style and growing success, making it my favorite release of 2026.

Method

How I ranked these albums

What I judged on

I ranked these albums based on their production, songwriting, creativity, replay value, and overall impact.

What I ignored

Streaming numbers and chart positions were not the only factors. I focused on which albums I personally enjoyed listening to the most.

Music is subjective, and everyone has different favorites. Even though some albums had bigger commercial success, my ranking reflects my personal taste.
